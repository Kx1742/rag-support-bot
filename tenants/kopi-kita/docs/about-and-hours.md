# About Kopi Kita, Hours and Location

## About Us
Kopi Kita is a modern kopitiam: old-school kopi and teh pulled by hand, nasi lemak, roti bakar and
kuih, in a bright café with air-conditioning and fast Wi-Fi. Kopi Kita is a fictional business used
for a demo.

## Opening Hours
- Monday to Friday: 7:00 AM – 10:00 PM
- Saturday and Sunday: 7:00 AM – 11:00 PM
- Public holidays: normal hours.
- Closed on the first two days of Chinese New Year (6 and 7 February 2027) and the first two days of
  Hari Raya Aidilfitri. We post exact holiday dates on our Instagram.

The kitchen stops taking food orders 30 minutes before closing. Drinks are served until closing.

## Location
12 Jalan Contoh 3, Petaling Jaya, Selangor (fictional address).
We are a 5-minute walk from the nearest LRT station.

## Parking and Access
Street parking with the MBPJ parking app or coupons; free parking after 6 PM and on Sundays.
Step-free entry from the five-foot way (kaki lima). We have one accessible restroom.

## Wi-Fi and Laptops
Free Wi-Fi; the password is on your receipt. Laptops are welcome, but please share tables during the
lunch rush (12:00 PM – 2:00 PM).

## Seating
Indoor air-conditioned tables, and outdoor tables on the kaki lima (five-foot way) for those who like
fresh air. Smoking is not allowed anywhere, including the kaki lima tables, by law.

## Table Bookings
You can book a table through this chat or the "Book a table" button, for up to 6 people, at least
1 hour ahead and up to 30 days in advance. Bookings last 90 minutes. Bigger groups: WhatsApp us.
