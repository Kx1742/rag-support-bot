# Kopi Points and Order Ahead

## Kopi Points (loyalty)
- Earn 1 Kopi Point for every RM1 you pay (including SST), at the counter or when you order ahead.
- Join for free at the counter with your phone number. No app needed.
- Rewards:
  - Free kuih – 20 points
  - Free kopi or teh (any drink from the Kopi & Teh menu) – 30 points
  - Free breakfast: nasi lemak + teh tarik – 90 points
- Tell the staff you want to use a reward when you pay. Check your points with the "My Kopi Points"
  button in this chat.
- Points expire if you don't visit for 12 months.

## Order Ahead (pickup)
- Use the "Order ahead" button in this chat. Pick your food and drinks, hot or iced, and how sweet.
- Choose a pickup time today, or "as soon as possible" (usually about 10 minutes).
- Pay when you collect, by cash, card or DuitNow QR. Order-ahead is takeaway, so there's no service
  charge; SST of 6% applies.
- You can follow your order live in the chat. Listen for your number on the screen at the counter.
- Up to 15 items per online order. For bigger orders or events, WhatsApp us.
