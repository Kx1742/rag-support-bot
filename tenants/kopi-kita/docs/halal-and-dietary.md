# Halal, Vegetarian and Allergies

## Is Kopi Kita halal?
Our kitchen is pork-free and we don't use lard or alcohol in any food or drink. Our chicken, eggs
and other ingredients come from halal-certified suppliers. Kopi Kita itself is not yet
JAKIM halal-certified; our application is in progress. We are Muslim-friendly and many of our
regulars are Muslim, but if you only eat at certified premises, please take note.

## Vegetarian and Vegan
- Vegetarian: roti bakar kaya butter, half-boiled eggs, kuih lapis and onde-onde.
- Our nasi lemak sambal contains ikan bilis (anchovies) and belacan (shrimp paste), so it is not
  vegetarian. Ask for nasi lemak without sambal and ikan bilis if you prefer.
- Vegan options are limited: Kopi O, Teh O Limau, Barley Limau, and onde-onde.

## Allergies
- Nuts: nasi lemak comes with peanuts. Tell us if you have a nut allergy and we'll leave them out,
  but our kitchen handles peanuts, so we can't promise no traces.
- Dairy: kopi, kopi C and teh tarik use condensed or evaporated milk; Kopi O and Teh O have no milk.
- Gluten: roti bakar and mee goreng contain wheat. Nasi lemak is gluten-free apart from the soy in
  some sambal brands; ask us.
- Seafood: sambal contains ikan bilis and belacan.
If you have a serious allergy, please tell the staff when ordering.

## Sugar
Malaysian kopi and teh are sweet. Ask for kurang manis (less sweet) or kosong (no sugar); there's no
extra charge.
