# Payments, SST and Service Charge

## SST and Service Charge
- Menu prices do not include tax.
- SST (Sales and Service Tax) of 6% is added to every order, for dine-in and takeaway.
- A 10% service charge is added to dine-in orders only. Takeaway and order-ahead have no service charge.
- SST is charged on the bill after the service charge, as the law requires.
- Example: a RM10.00 dine-in order is RM10.00 + RM1.00 service charge + RM0.66 SST = RM11.66.
  The same order as takeaway is RM10.00 + RM0.60 SST = RM10.60.
- Your receipt shows the service charge and SST separately.

## Ways to Pay
- Cash.
- Debit and credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, contactless).
- DuitNow QR: scan the QR code at the counter with your banking app or e-wallet (Touch 'n Go
  eWallet, GrabPay, Boost and most Malaysian banking apps). Show the staff the payment success screen.
- We don't accept cheques or foreign currency.

## Cash Rounding
Cash payments are rounded to the nearest 5 sen, following Bank Negara Malaysia's rounding
mechanism. For example, RM11.66 is paid as RM11.65 and RM11.68 as RM11.70. Card and DuitNow QR
payments are charged the exact amount. The rounding is shown on your receipt.

## Refunds
If something is wrong with your order, tell us at the counter and we'll fix it or refund it.
Refunds go back the same way you paid (cash, card or DuitNow). Card and DuitNow refunds can take a few
working days to appear, depending on your bank.

## Receipts and e-Invoices
You get a receipt for every paid order, printed or on your phone if you ordered ahead. If your company
needs an e-Invoice, ask at the counter with your company details.
