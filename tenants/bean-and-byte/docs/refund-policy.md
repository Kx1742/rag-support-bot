# Refund and Order Policy

## Drinks and Food
If your drink or food is not made correctly, tell us within 15 minutes and we will remake
it for free or give you a full refund.

## Online Orders
Online orders can be cancelled for a full refund up to 10 minutes after they are placed.
After that, the order has usually been started and cannot be refunded, but we will
always help if something went wrong.

## Gift Cards
Gift cards cannot be refunded or exchanged for cash. They never expire and can be used
for any item, including online orders. Lost gift cards can be replaced if you have the
original receipt.

## Catering Orders
Catering orders can be cancelled for a full refund up to 48 hours before the pickup or
delivery time. Cancellations within 48 hours receive a 50% refund.

## How to Request a Refund
Speak to staff in the café, or email hello@beanandbyte.example with your receipt or
order number. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days.
