- Americano — $3.50
- Flat White — $4.50
- Cappuccino — $4.50
- Caffè Latte — $4.75 (hot or iced)
- Mocha — $5.25

## Not Coffee

- Fresh Orange Juice — $4.50

## Sizes and Options
Most drinks come in Regular or Large (Large is $0.70 more). Any coffee can be made decaf or iced at no extra charge.

## Milk Options
Whole milk is standard. Oat, almond and soy milk are available for an extra $0.60.
An extra espresso shot costs $0.80.
Lactose-free milk is available at no extra charge.

- Breakfast Bagel with egg and cheddar — $8.00 (vegetarian)
- Chicken Pesto Panini — $11.00
- Banana Bread — $3.75 (contains nuts)
- Gluten-free Brownie — $4.00

## Allergens
Our kitchen handles nuts, gluten, dairy, eggs and soy. We cannot guarantee any item is
completely allergen-free. Please tell staff about allergies before ordering.
