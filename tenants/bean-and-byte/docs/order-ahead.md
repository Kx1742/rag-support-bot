# Order Ahead

You can order ahead for pickup with the "Order ahead" button in this chat.
Choose your items and options, enter your name and phone number, and pick a time
(as soon as possible, or a later time today while we're open). Orders are usually ready
in about 10 minutes.

You pay when you collect your order, by cash or card. Watch the pickup screen at the counter
for your order number. If you're a Byte Points member, use the same phone number and the
order earns points when you pay.

Online orders can have up to 20 items. For bigger orders, please see our catering service.
To change or cancel an online order, please call us.
