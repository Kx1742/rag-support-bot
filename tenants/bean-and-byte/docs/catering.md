# Catering

We cater office meetings, parties and events for 10 to 150 people.

## Packages
- Coffee Box: serves 10 cups — $28 (includes cups, milk and sugar)
- Breakfast Platter: assorted croissants and banana bread for 10 people — $45
- Lunch Platter: assorted paninis for 10 people — $95 (vegetarian options available)

## Ordering
Please order at least 24 hours in advance. For orders over 50 people, order at least
3 days in advance. Order by email at catering@beanandbyte.example or call (555) 010-2041.

## Delivery
Free delivery within 5 km for orders over $100. Otherwise delivery costs $10.
Pickup is available during opening hours.
