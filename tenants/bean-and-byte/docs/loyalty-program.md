# Byte Points Loyalty Program

## How It Works
Join for free in our app or at the counter with your phone number.
You earn 1 Byte Point for every $1 you spend.

## Rewards
- 50 points: free pastry
- 100 points: free drink of any size
- 250 points: free breakfast combo (any coffee + any food item)

## Extras
- Birthday reward: a free drink during your birthday week.
- Bring your own cup and earn 5 bonus points per drink.
- Points expire after 12 months without any purchase.

## Rules
Points cannot be earned on gift card purchases. Points cannot be transferred between
accounts or exchanged for cash.

## Checking Your Points
Tap "My points" in this chat and enter the phone number you joined with to see your balance.
Show your phone number at the counter to earn points or use a reward.
