# Hours and Location

## Opening Hours
- Monday to Friday: 7:00 AM – 6:00 PM
- Saturday: 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM
- Sunday: 9:00 AM – 3:00 PM
- Public holidays: 9:00 AM – 2:00 PM. Closed on Christmas Day and New Year's Day.

The kitchen stops serving hot food 30 minutes before closing.

## Location
42 Circuit Lane, Maple District (fictional address).
We are a 3-minute walk from Maple Station.

## Parking and Access
Free street parking is available for up to 2 hours. The café is wheelchair accessible,
with step-free entry and an accessible restroom.

## Wi-Fi and Laptops
Free Wi-Fi is available for customers. The password is printed on your receipt.
Laptops are welcome, but during the weekday lunch rush (12:00 PM – 2:00 PM) we ask
laptop users to limit table time to 90 minutes.

## Pets
Dogs are welcome on the outdoor patio. Only service animals are allowed inside.
